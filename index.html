001 — About

What is
ANIS?

ANIS is a multimodal human-computer interaction system that fuses voice, large language models, and bio-signal eye tracking into one seamless, hands-free interface.

Built for the future of accessibility and productivity — where your voice, gaze, and intent become the keyboard, mouse, and command line.

007 — Purpose

Designed for
Human Limitation

For many people, interacting with a computer isn’t simple — it’s impossible. Motor impairments can take away the ability to type, to click, to even hold a device.

But one ability often remains longer than the rest — eye movement. Even in advanced stages of motor impairment, the eyes can still communicate intent.

ANIS was built around that reality. A system where voice, gaze, and minimal movement become enough — where interaction doesn’t depend on strength, but on presence.

This isn’t just about convenience. It’s about restoring control, independence, and a way to interact with the world — when traditional interfaces fail.
